# Exercise catalog credits

The bundled exercise library in `src/lib/server/exercises/catalog.ts` and its
images in `static/exercise-assets/` are derived from the
[free-exercise-db](https://github.com/yuhonas/free-exercise-db) dataset
([Unlicense](http://unlicense.org/) / public domain). Images were resized and
converted to WebP; exercise metadata (names, muscle groups, equipment,
instructions) comes from the same dataset.
